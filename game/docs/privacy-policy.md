## Information the game stores
Your scores, coins, boosters, settings and saved game are stored **only on your device**. We do not run servers that receive this data, and we do not ask for your name, email or any account.

## Advertising
The game shows ads through **Google AdMob**. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:
- device identifiers such as the advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking);
- approximate location derived from your IP address;
- information about ad interactions, device type, operating system and app diagnostics.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

**Your choices**
- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent on first launch. You can change it at any time in **Settings → Privacy options**.
- iOS: you can allow or deny tracking in the system prompt, and later in *Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking*.
- Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in *Settings → Google → Ads*.

## Children
